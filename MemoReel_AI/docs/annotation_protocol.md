# Preference annotation protocol (draft; not approved for collection)

**Purpose:** compare two generated edits of the same consented source under the same declared mood/style request.

**Interaction:** show source and candidates with randomized left/right order. Ask “Which result better matches the requested mood and your taste while keeping the important parts of the original?” Allow “no preference / neither” in the eventual study interface to reduce forced choices. Collect optional reason without prompting for sensitive personal details.

**Stored fields:** anonymous participant ID, source ID, candidate IDs, requested mood, selected aesthetic, winner/tie, optional reason, sequence or timestamp, consent version/reference, and generation configuration references. Avoid direct identifiers in research exports. Keep raw photos in separate private storage with explicit deletion and access controls.

**Governance prerequisites:** obtain institutional ethics review/determination; define participant eligibility, consent/withdrawal, image ownership and third-party depiction rules, retention period, access roles, incident procedure, deletion propagation and whether any images may be used for model training (default: no). Pilot comprehension and annotation quality before a powered study. No recruitment or collection under this draft.
