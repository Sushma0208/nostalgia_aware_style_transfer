# Novelty assessment (provisional)

The broad idea “learn preferences and use them to personalize diffusion generation” is not novel by itself: ImageReward and diffusion preference alignment establish general preference learning, and a 2025 paper explicitly studies personalized preference fine-tuning of diffusion models. A 2026 search result also indicates emotion stylization with explicit reasoning and preference optimization; it needs primary-source verification.

Potentially defensible empirical contribution: a carefully controlled study of few-shot user preference ranking for edits conditioned on the user's own source photo and intended mood, measuring preference separately from content preservation, emotion alignment, and explanation accuracy. This remains a research gap hypothesis until systematic literature review and feasibility study.

Decision: proceed only with a small ranking baseline as the first experiment; do not promise a novel generation algorithm. If literature overlap is strong, position contribution as evaluation/data protocol or adapt the question based on supervisor review.
