# Literature review and search strategy (initial)

Search date: 2026-10-13 (client date). Coverage is an initial verified seed set, not exhaustive. Search across CVF Open Access, PMLR, arXiv, ACL Anthology and primary project documentation using combinations of “emotion guided image generation”, “affective image stylization”, “personalized image generation preference”, “pairwise preference diffusion”, “image conditioned diffusion preservation”, and “explainable generative AI”. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.13438) |

The 2025 personalized preference fine-tuning work means the project must not claim novelty for preference-based diffusion personalization alone. Closest baseline currently: participant-conditioned pairwise ranking / preference fine-tuning. Proposed distinction is the evaluated setting (source-conditioned nostalgic edits, low-feedback ranking, separate intent and content preservation measures), but this is provisional until a broader search finds overlaps. A relevant 2026 result was surfaced in search (“LLaVA-Emo”, Electronics, 2026) combining affective stylization, explicit style reasoning, and preference optimization; its source and claims must be independently assessed in the next literature pass before fixing the gap.
